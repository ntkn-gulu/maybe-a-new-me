# Architecture — Maybe A New Me

> Map of the code as it is now.

## Current state
A bilingual (Vietnamese / English) PWA with a tactile "warm paper + extruded objects" design system, synthesized sounds and experimental iOS haptics.

- **Focus** runs real strict-mode sessions: the equipped timer skin's dial (classic, Retro LCD, Nixie, Neon or Hourglass), countdown on a black screen in the skin's colors, wake lock, leave-the-app limits (phone), random "Still there?" checks with an escalating chime, a required note, and saved history.
- **Sound room** (a Focus sub-page: tap the active Focus tab again): an ambient mixer. Nature and places are CC0 field recordings (BigSoundBank), downloaded the first time they play and then kept offline; wind, campfire crackles and brown noise are synthesized. Free: rain, wind, waves, campfire, brown noise; from chests (marked with a small chest, not locked yet): night field, creek, countryside, café, thunderstorm, night train. A play key with what plays and the mix volume, six ready-made mixes, and a grid of tiles: a tap turns a sound on (up to 5 at once), a sideways drag sets its volume with a tick every 10%; each tile's little scene moves while it plays. The mix is saved; playing starts again only from a tap.
- **Journey** shows the equipped title as a name tag, the real streak (from session history) with the theme's flame for every reached tier (tiers not reached yet hide their color and name behind a "?"), a sport-day check-in card on sport days, best streak + streak freezes, and a month calendar heatmap (kept / frozen / missed / today).
- **Level up**: reaching a new tier opens a full-screen moment once after any session screen closes: the old flame shakes, the user taps it 3 times (each tap buzzes the iPhone and cracks it), it bursts and the new one assembles.
- **Streak lost**: when a streak has just been broken, a gentle card shows once (the flame out with a wisp of smoke, how long the streak was, the best streak and items kept, a Streak Freeze tip, "Light it again today"); when a freeze saved yesterday, Journey says so in a small ice-blue note.
- **Onboarding** (first run, full screen): in a phone browser first how to add the app to the home screen (iPhone: the Share steps, since Safari and the home screen app keep separate data; Android: a one-tap install when the browser offers it, else the menu steps of that browser: Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet, Firefox), then welcome, the flame levels up, chests, daily goal, sport days and the strict rules. Finishing applies the goal and sport days (from today on a fresh install). People who already used the app skip it; Settings can replay it.
- **Settings**: daily goal (default 2) and sport days (both apply tomorrow), replay the intro, language (also on the first onboarding screen), date format (DD/MM or MM/DD), sound, haptics, and Your data: save a backup file (share sheet on a phone, download on a computer; the caption shows the last backup day) and restore one after a confirmation that shows what the file holds; dev mode also shows a theme preview (Equipped / 3D / Sketch), "try every item" (equip anything without owning it), the sound test and "+10 chests".
- **Themes** come from the equipped theme item (`inventory_store.equipped.theme`): **3D** (`theme_classic` → `classic_3d`, default: extruded tiles, low-poly flame, extruded dial and chest; the Rare color themes Midnight, Matcha, Sunset and Ocean are palettes on it, Midnight a dark one) and **Sketch** (Legendary `theme_sketch` → `sketch`: the whole app drawn in a sketchbook: Patrick Hand font, wobbly ink frames, hatched pen shadows, a marker + ink flame). Dev mode can preview any theme without owning it (`settings_store.dev_theme`). Each illustration has an extruded and a sketch version, picked with `useArtStyle()`. Adding a new drawing-style theme follows `theme_checklist.md`. The git tag `theme-3d-original` marks the last commit before the sketch work.
- **Chests**: keeping a day gives a daily chest (plus a silver one every 7th day in a row and a milestone chest with the new tier's flame color on a new tier), once per day; a fresh install starts with 3 welcome chests. On the Rewards tab a tap opens the oldest waiting chest in a full-screen opening (tap 3 times to crack it, burst in the rarity color, a dark build-up and gold burst for legendary) that shows the item (new, or a duplicate turned into Flame Shards) with Equip for items that already work (flame colors, finished themes). With two or more waiting, "Open N at once" (up to 10) opens them as one gacha pull: the chest glows and cracks in the color of the best drop, then the items come out one at a time, rarest last (Next or tap; Skip), ending on a summary of the whole haul.
- **Collection** (a Rewards sub-page: tap the active Rewards tab again and pick it from the page stack): every item by slot (flame colors, timer skins, sound packs, themes, titles) with owned counts; owned items show their picture and an equipped tick, missing ones a "?" with their rarity, shard price or the streak that grants them (milestone flame names stay hidden). A tap opens a detail card: Equip, "Listen" for sound packs, a note when a 3D-only timer skin is seen in the sketch theme, or trading Flame Shards for a missing gacha item (tap twice to trade).
- **Equipped items** all change the app: flame color (Journey and level-up flame), timer skin (Focus dial + running countdown colors), sound pack (every tap sound), theme (drawing style or color palette), title (name tag on Journey).
- Every tap target is a `div` with button semantics (`getPressableProps`) holding a `HapticOverlay`, so iPhones buzz on tap.
- `src/core/` holds all game rules as pure, tested TypeScript.
- Hidden dev mode (tap the Focus kicker 5 times): sessions run 25x faster (time limits stay in real seconds); Journey shows a dev panel to fake the streak (0–400), try flame color items, replay the level-up screen and preview the sport check-in card on any day.
- Flames support an equipped color item (`color_id`); nothing equips one yet outside the dev panel.

Time, time zone and randomness are always passed in, so every rule is deterministic and tested.

| File | Role |

## Data flow
- **Session:** Focus start → `focus_store.start` → core `startSession`. `useFocusGuard` feeds ticks and visibility events → core state machine → new `session`. On break or completion, `focus_store` writes a `SessionRecord` to `progress_store`.
- **Progress:** `progress_store` history → core `buildDayRecords` (`useDayRecords`) → `computeStreak` → Journey (streak, tier, freezes, calendar) and Focus/Done screens (today's done / goal / sessions to keep).
- **Sport day:** Journey `SportCheckIn` → `progress_store.checkInSport` → day record `sport_checked_in` → `isDayKept` needs only `SPORT_DAY_MIN_SESSIONS`.
- **Level up:** `useStreak().current_tier_index` vs stored `celebrated_tier_index` → `getLevelUp` → `LevelUpGate` shows `LevelUpOverlay` → closing it saves the tier.
- **Settings:** `settings_store` → subscription → `sfx_engine` / `haptic_hack`; `language` → `useT()` → every visible string; `date_format` → `formatDayMonth` in the calendar. Onboarding / Settings → `progress_store` goal and sport weekday schedules. Backup: Settings → `backup_storage` → core `backup` ⇄ localStorage (restore reloads every store).
- **Ambient:** Sound room tile / preset / play → `ambient_store` (core `ambient_mix`) → subscription → `ambient_engine.setAmbientTarget` → recipes (`ambient_layers` + `ambient_kit`: recordings via `ambient_files` (service worker cache "audio"), crackles from `ambient_tracks` via `ambient_dsp`) → own bus → speakers. `audio_context.onContextChanged` → layers rebuilt on the new context.
- **Flame visuals:** active theme → `useArtStyle()` → `Flame` (`sketch_flame` or `low_poly_flame`) ← tier index (+ optional color item id) via `getFlameColors` / `getFlameEffects`; `sketch_flame_art` + `sketch_draw` → the sketch flame's geometry; `TIER_COLORS` → `tier_strip`.
- **Theme:** `inventory_store.equipped.theme` (+ `settings_store.dev_theme` in dev mode) → `getActiveTheme` / `getActivePalette` (`active_theme.ts`) → `<html data-theme>` (→ `sketch_theme.css` restyles every element tagged with the `sk-*` classes; `useArtStyle()` picks each illustration) and `<html data-palette>` (→ `palettes.css` swaps the tokens).
- **Equipped items:** `inventory_store.equipped` → flame color (`getFlameColors`), `useTimerSkin()` (Focus dial, running colors), `setSoundPack` (subscription → `playSfx`), `TitleBadge`.
- **Chests:** today kept (`useTodayProgress`) + `useStreak` → `RewardGate` → `inventory_store.rewardKeptDay` → `pending_chests`. Rewards tab tap (one) or "Open N at once" → `openChests` → core `openChestBatch` → `openChest` (rates + pity) → item or shards → `ChestOpeningOverlay` → `equip`. Rewards tab tapped again → `CollectionScreen` → `getItemOffer` → `ItemSheet` → `equip` / `buyWithShards` (core `exchangeShards`).

## Conventions
- Variables `snake_case`, functions `camelCase`, components/types/enums `PascalCase`, constants `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE`.
- Files `snake_case`, including React component files (`tab_bar.tsx` exports `TabBar`). CSS Module class names are `snake_case`.
- Component props use `snake_case` fields (`is_selected`, `aria_label`); callback props are `camelCase` (`onChange`).
- Tap targets: `div` + `getPressableProps` + `<HapticOverlay />`, never `<button>` (except `HoldButton`).
- Navigation: the tab bar always has exactly 4 tabs. A new screen is never a 5th tab but a sub-page of an existing tab: tapping the already-active tab pops up a stack of its other pages to pick from (the tab itself shows the page that is open), and a tab that has sub-pages shows a small hint mark. Today: Rewards → Collection, Focus → Sound room. A tab gets one by adding it to the tab's `sub_pages` in `TABS` (a `SubPageId`) and a case in `renderSubPage` (`app.tsx`).
- Tap targets inside scrolling lists (like the Collection cards) get no `HapticOverlay`: on iOS a swipe that starts on the switch is taken by the switch, so the page does not scroll and a tap fires.
- Framed UI (tiles, buttons, switches, pips, cells, ribbons, inputs) carries the global `sk-*` classes so the sketch theme can redraw it; pick the fill with `--sk-fill` in the component CSS. Every illustration has an extruded version (3D theme) and a sketch version built from `sketch_draw.ts` geometry, picked with `useArtStyle()`.
- Smoothness on iPhone: never put a live SVG filter in the page and never use gradient patterns for textures. Hand-drawn art is plain vector geometry from `sketch_draw.ts` (sharp at any size, as cheap as the 3D polygons); animate it with transform/opacity, give moving parts their own SVG / GPU layer, and keep `pathLength` draw-ons to short intros.
- Never animate opacity with a bouncy spring (it overshoots and brings ghosts back); pair springs with `OPACITY_FADE`.
- Zustand: select single values (or functions) per hook call; never return a new object from a selector (Zustand 5 would re-render forever).
- Allman braces; English code comments with aligned trailing `//` comments.
- All visible text comes from `src/i18n/strings.ts` via `useT()` (both `vi` and `en`); "VI"/"EN" and "DD/MM"/"MM/DD" labels and the "Tiếng Việt · English" caption are the only literals. Display and mono text is uppercased with CSS (`text-transform`), not in the source strings.
- Lint with ESLint + @stylistic; Prettier is not used.
